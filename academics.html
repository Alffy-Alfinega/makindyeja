Our Academic Approach

At Makindye Junior Academy, we follow a comprehensive curriculum that combines the Ugandan national curriculum with international best practices in primary education. Our approach focuses on developing critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills while ensuring academic excellence.

Nursery Program

Ages 3-5 Years

Our nursery program provides a warm, nurturing environment where young children can explore, learn, and grow through play-based activities and structured learning experiences.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Early literacy and numeracy skills
  • Social and emotional development
  • Fine and gross motor skills
  • Creative expression through art and music
  • Basic science exploration
Daily Activities Include:
  • Story time and reading
  • Number games and counting
  • Art and craft activities
  • Music and movement
  • Outdoor play and exploration

Lower Primary

Primary 1-3 (Ages 6-8 Years)

Our lower primary program builds strong foundations in core subjects while encouraging curiosity and love for learning. We focus on developing essential skills that prepare students for advanced learning.

Core Subjects:
  • English Language and Literature
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Social Studies
  • Religious Education
Additional Subjects:
  • Physical Education
  • Art and Design
  • Music
  • Computer Literacy

Upper Primary

Primary 4-7 (Ages 9-12 Years)

Our upper primary program prepares students for secondary education and national examinations. We emphasize critical thinking, problem-solving, and independent learning skills.

Core Subjects:
  • English Language and Literature
  • Mathematics
  • Integrated Science
  • Social Studies
  • Religious Education
Preparation Focus:
  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) preparation
  • Study skills and time management
  • Research and presentation skills
  • Leadership development
  • Career guidance

Extracurricular Activities

We believe in holistic development through diverse extracurricular activities that complement academic learning.

Sports

Football, basketball, volleyball, athletics, and swimming to promote physical fitness and teamwork.

Music

Vocal training, instrument lessons, school choir, and traditional music appreciation.

Art

Drawing, painting, sculpture, and craft activities to nurture creativity and self-expression.

Debate Club

Public speaking, critical thinking, and argumentation skills development through debates.

ICT Club

Computer skills, coding basics, digital literacy, and technology integration in learning.

Reading Club

Literature appreciation, reading comprehension, and creative writing workshops.

Environmental Club

Nature conservation, gardening, recycling projects, and environmental awareness.

Drama

Acting, script writing, stage performance, and school play productions.

Science Laboratory

Computer Laboratory

School Library

Extensive collection of books and learning resources.
