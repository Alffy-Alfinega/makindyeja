Join Our School Family

We welcome you to join Makindye Junior Academy, where we nurture young minds for a brighter future. Our admission process is designed to be simple and transparent.

Admission Process

1

Obtain Application Form

Visit our school office or download the application form from our website. Forms are available throughout the year.

2

Complete and Submit Form

Fill in all required information accurately and attach necessary documents. Submit the completed form to the school office.

3

Assessment Interview

Applicants will be scheduled for an age-appropriate assessment and interview with our academic team.

4

Admission Decision

Successful applicants will receive admission letters within one week of the assessment.

5

Complete Registration

Pay the required fees and complete all registration formalities to secure your child's place.

Entry Requirements

Nursery (Ages 3-5)

  • Birth certificate or copy
  • Immunization card
  • 2 passport size photographs
  • Parent/guardian ID copy
  • Previous school reports (if applicable)

Primary (Ages 6-12)

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school reports/transcripts
  • Transfer certificate (from previous school)
  • 2 passport size photographs
  • Parent/guardian ID copy
  • Immunization card

Additional Information

  • Students must meet the minimum age requirements for each class level
  • Assessment tests are conducted to determine appropriate class placement
  • Medical examination report may be required for special needs assessment
  • All documents must be submitted in original or certified copies

Payment Information

  • Fees are payable termly in advance
  • Payment methods: Bank transfer, Mobile money, Cash at school office
  • Bank details available at the school office
  • Late payment attracts a 10% penalty after the first two weeks of term

Scholarships & Financial Aid

We believe that quality education should be accessible to all deserving students. Makindye Junior Academy offers limited scholarships and financial assistance to eligible students.

Academic Excellence

Merit-based scholarships for top-performing students in national examinations.

Financial Need

Need-based financial assistance for families facing economic challenges.

Talent Recognition

Special scholarships for students with exceptional talents in sports, arts, or music.

How to Apply for Scholarships

  • Submit a scholarship application form along with admission documents
  • Provide proof of financial need (for need-based scholarships)
  • Include academic records and achievement certificates
  • Attend a scholarship interview with the scholarship committee
  • Scholarship decisions are communicated within two weeks of application
